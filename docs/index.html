Policies

Returns
Unopened, intact vials may be returned within 14 days of delivery for replacement or refund of the reagent cost. Opened or temperature-abused materials cannot be accepted. Failed-in-analysis materials are replaced when the independent document supports the claim.
Shipping
Domestic carriers, ambient temperature, sealed secondary packaging with the research-use statement. We ship to laboratory and institutional addresses.
What we do not sell
We do not sell compounds that duplicate FDA-approved drug molecules. We do not sell administration supplies or diluents of any kind. We do not sell to individuals for personal use.
Privacy
Customer organization information is used solely for order processing and regulatory documentation. We never sell or share contact data.
