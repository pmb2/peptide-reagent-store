Quality & Testing

  • Every lot is documented with lot-specific HPLC chromatography and mass-spectrometry identity testing performed by an independent analytical laboratory.
  • The vial lot number, certificate of analysis, and lab report lot number always match.
  • Lots that do not pass independent verification are never sold. They are documented and destroyed.
  • Retain samples of every received lot are archived.
  • Materials are stored sealed, protected from light and moisture until dispatch.

Ordering

We supply verified research organizations, laboratories, and principal investigators. Orders are processed as quotes: submit your request and our team confirms availability of the current tested lot, shipping, and payment by institutional purchase order, wire/ACH, or other approved laboratory payment methods.

  • No consumer sales. No personal-use orders. Orders that cannot describe an institutional research context are cancelled and refunded.
  • We do not sell reagents that duplicate FDA-approved drug molecules.
  • We do not sell administration supplies of any kind.
  • Shipping: domestic carriers, ambient temperature, sealed secondary packaging.
