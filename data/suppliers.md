## Red flags (from conversation)
- Stock COA PDF with no lot number → walk away
- Won't name plant city → walk away
- Sells GLP-1s or offers to add them → walk away
- Offers BAC water / syringes in same box → walk away
- Asks for full wire up front on first order → split or escrow, treat as loss risk

## RFQ requirements (every email must ask)
1. Current-lot HPLC chromatogram + MS identity (images or PDF, lot-matched)
2. Manufacturing plant city and facility type
3. 10-vial pilot order pricing incl. DHL/FedEx to US, per-SKU
4. Price tiers at 10 / 50 vials
5. Whether they can ship with OUR label file (we print): distributor = our LLC,
   "FOR LABORATORY RESEARCH USE ONLY / NOT FOR HUMAN OR ANIMAL USE"
6. Quality agreement / no-resale-of-brand willingness
